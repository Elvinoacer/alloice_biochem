## Brand & Style

This design system lives at the convergence of field botany, East African plant biodiversity, and molecular biochemistry. It rejects the sterilized, clinical coldness of conventional pharmaceutical software in favor of an organic, scientific editorial aesthetic that feels luminous, breathing, and rigorous. The interface operates like a master researcher's living folio: combining deep canopy greens, luminous enzymatic bioluminescence, and warm amber resin accents with meticulous typographic discipline.

The target audience spans phytochemists, botanical pharmacologists, biotech pioneers, and high-tier institutional researchers who require exact molecular data alongside rich, qualitative biological narratives.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Form
The visual language fuses **Scientific Editorial Precision** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Tactile Bio-Surfaces**: The dark foundation mirrors deep subterranean humus and dense indigenous forest canopies, punctuated by cellular, translucent layers that evoke microscope glass slides, petri dishes, and biological wet mounts.
- **Specimen Hierarchy**: Generous whitespace alternates with densely structured data modules, balancing authoritative academic prose against precise spectroscopic telemetry.
- **Living Phosphorescence**: Interactive moments produce gentle enzymatic blooms—subtle bioluminescent halos rather than synthetic neon glows.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in photosynthetic and secondary plant metabolite chemistry. It leverages high-contrast luminescence on top of deep forest slate and chloroplastic depths.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#10b981`) — Cellular Bioluminescence**: Denotes active molecular states, living confirmations, key interactive vectors, and vital pathways.
- **Secondary (`#f59e0b`) — Polyphenol Amber**: Used exclusively for bioactive highlights, extraction yields, metabolic flags, and secondary compounds (e.g., flavonoids, alkaloids, resinous fractions).
- **Tertiary (`#064e3b`) — Canopy Emerald**: Forms mid-ground structural surfaces, container boundaries, and active matrix cards.
- **Neutral (`#022c22`) — Earthen Bedrock Slate**: The foundational canvas base, preventing pure-black voiding and sustaining organic chromatic grounding.

### Functional & Surface Roles
- **Canvas Base**: `#021a14` to `#022c22` for the viewport backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Petri Tier)**: `#04362b` with a 1px perimeter of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Specimen Mount)**: `#064e3b` with a 1px perimeter of `rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.08)`.
- **Dewdrop White (`#f0fdf4` / `#ffffff`)**: Reserved for high-priority editorial headers, quantitative metrics, and crystalline readouts.
- **Subdued Bio-Text**: `rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.65)` for scientific methodology body copy and contextual meta-descriptions.
- **Enzymatic Warning / Mutation**: `#fb7185` (Carotenoid/Phenolic stress) applied sparingly for cytotoxic alerts or assay variances.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary cadence of an academic monograph with the computational exactitude of spectroscopic instruments.

### Font Roles
- **Editorial Voice (`Newsreader`)**: Drives all headlines, section preludes, scholarly narratives, and contextual footnotes. Rendered with optical sizing when available; italic weights denote Latin binomial nomenclature (*e.g., Warburgia ugandensis*) and field voucher annotations.
- **Instrument Precision (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all quantitative data, chromatography readouts, chemical formulas, assay timestamps, interactive control triggers, and taxonomic identifiers. It ensures numbers remain tabular and data densities align impeccably across laboratory viewports.

### Style Directives
- **Taxonomic Nomenclature**: Always set plant genera and species in `Newsreader Italic` with baseline tracking.
- **Telemetry Readouts**: Always set assay values, concentration yields (`µg/mL`), and coordinates in `JetBrains Mono` with uppercase styling and expanded tracking.
- **Leading & Rhythm**: Headlines intentionally utilize tightened line heights to emphasize scholarly gravitas, while body narrative uses open spacing (`1.6x` line-height) to prevent eye fatigue during lengthy compound reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a multi-tier **Compound Fluid Grid** designed to mirror lab bench partitioning and field notebooks. 

### Grid Geometry & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column dynamic fluid grid. Outer section margins set to `margin-lg` (`4rem`) with interior gutters of `gutter-lg` (`2rem`). Supports split editorial views: a 7-column analytical narrative paired with a 5-column molecular visualizer or raw data stream.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid with `margin` (`2rem`) and `gutter` (`1.5rem`). Secondary assay tables collapse beneath the main thesis block.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column linear layout. Margins tighten to `margin-sm` (`1rem`) with `gutter-sm` (`1rem`). Data ribbons, telemetry bars, and compound cards stack linearly into single-column laboratory strips.

### Spatial Rhythms
Spacing relies on strict vertical multiples:
- Micro gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) bind label tags directly to their numerical data readouts.
- Structural component padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`) provides generous breathing room within glass containers, simulating specimens isolated within distinct optical wells.
- Module margins (`space-xl`) separate distinct analytical sections (*e.g., Ethnobotanical Context vs. Mass Spectrometry Breakdown*).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque grey dropshadows; depth is generated through **Optical Specimen Layering** (semi-transparent tinted glass surfaces and bioluminescent ambient light diffusion).

### Surface Depth Layers
1. **Bedrock Canvas (Ground 0)**: Non-reflective base of `#021a14`. Provides absolute contrast for enzymatic elements.
2. **Glass Mount (Layer 1)**: `background: rgba(4, 54, 43, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle capillary boundary `border: 1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
3. **Floating Cell / Specimen Focus (Layer 2)**: `background: rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.85)` with a delicate top edge highlight `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.20)`.

### Ambient Phosphorescent Halos
Interactive and active elements emit diffuse chloroplastic radiance:
- **Active Compound Glow**: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
- **Bioactive Amber Bloom**: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.30)`.
- **Inactive / Quiescent Containers**: No shadow; spatial distinction relies purely on the 1px low-contrast organic outline (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the **Soft (`1`)** classification. Elements borrow geometry from laboratory glassware, microscope cover slips, and crystalline molecular latices.

### Radius Assignments
- **Containers, Cards, & Vials**: `0.25rem` (`4px`) on base structures, escalating to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) on primary specimen analytical boards. This retains crisp, clinical discipline without brutalist harshness.
- **Pills, Chips, & Bio-Indicators**: Scaled up to `rounded-full` exclusively for micro status pills (*e.g., active enzymatic states, extraction fractions*) to reflect cellular morphology.
- **Input Fields & Bench Selectors**: Strictly `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`), delivering a surgical, scientific instrument feel.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Bio-Action (Synthesize / Extract)**:
  - Background: Solid cellular emerald (`#10b981`), text in deep earthen slate (`#022c22`), font `JetBrains Mono` weight 600, uppercase.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#34d399` with a subtle bioluminescent glow (`0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
  - Corner: `rounded-sm` (4px).
- **Secondary Assay (Phenolic Action)**:
  - Background: Transparent with 1px border of `#f59e0b`. Text `#f59e0b`.
  - Hover: `background: rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary Utility**:
  - Background: Invisible. Text `rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.7)`. Borderless with an underline accent on focus.

### Chips & Botanical Badges
- Constructed with `rounded-full` geometry.
- Base: `background: rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.45)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- Typography: `label-sm` in `JetBrains Mono`.
- Prefixed with an illuminated biological indicator: a 6px circular dot filled with either bioluminescent green (`#10b981`) for confirmed bioactivity or warm amber (`#f59e0b`) for active polyphenol screening.

### Specimen Cards & Analytical Wells
- Base panel: `background: rgba(4, 54, 43, 0.70)`, backdrop-filter `blur(12px)`.
- Borders: `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.14)`.
- Padding: `space-lg` internal clearance.
- Structure: Header containing Latin binomial nomenclature (`Newsreader` Italic) paired with laboratory accession number (`JetBrains Mono` right-aligned). Body carries molecular diagrams or quantitative assays.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#021f18` inset surface.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- Typography: User input in `Newsreader` (20px), placeholder text in `JetBrains Mono` (`12px`, uppercase, `rgba(240, 253, 244, 0.35)`).
- Active Focus: Border transitions to `#10b981` with an inner ring shadow of `0 0 0 1px #10b981`.

### Checkboxes & Discrete Switches
- Size: 16px × 16px square with 2px corners (`rounded-xs`).
- Unchecked: `#021a14` base with 1px border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- Checked: Fill `#10b981` with a dark checkmark `#022c22`.
- Radios: Circular 16px ring with a centered 6px glowing nucleus (`#10b981`) upon selection.

### Scientific Data Lists
- Alternating subtle rows with borders divided by `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.
- Left-hand identifier set in `Newsreader` body text; right-hand quantitative units (`IC50`, `m/z`, retention times) strictly aligned via `JetBrains Mono`. Hovering highlights the row with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.04)`.

### Specialized Laboratory Additions
- **Chromatography Peak Strip**: A continuous horizontal telemetry bar showing fractional purity; highlighted via an amber-to-emerald gradient with monospace peak callouts.
- **Herbarium Voucher Module**: Editorial media frame pairing macro photographic textures of leaves/bark with botanical field collection tags and voucher IDs.
